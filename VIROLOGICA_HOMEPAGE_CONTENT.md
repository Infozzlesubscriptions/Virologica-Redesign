## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### Exact live heading

## Frequently Asked Question

### 12.1 What does viral testing involve, and why is it important?

> Viral testing involves detecting and analysing viruses to assess product safety, performance, or contamination levels. It’s essential for medical devices, pharmaceuticals, and research projects to ensure compliance with regulatory standards and maintain product integrity. At Virologica, our viral testing services are conducted by experienced virologists using ISO/IEC 17025-accredited methods.

### 12.2 What makes Virologica a trusted viral testing lab in the UK?

> Virologica stands out as a trusted viral testing lab because each project is led by a PhD-qualified scientist, ensuring expert oversight from start to finish. Our ISO 17025 accreditation and transparent reporting processes reinforce our commitment to precision, quality, and scientific integrity.

### 12.3 Do you offer customised virology testing services for specific project needs?

> Yes, we provide fully customised virology testing services tailored to meet your exact requirements. Whether you need standard viral assays or bespoke testing methods, our experienced virologists ensure reliable, data-driven results aligned with the latest international standards.

### 12.4 How do your microbiology testing services support different industries?

> Our microbiology testing lab supports multiple industries including medical devices, biocides, wound care, and PPE. We offer both standard and customised microbiology testing services to help clients ensure product safety, efficacy, and compliance with regulatory frameworks.

### 12.5 What are biocompatibility testing services, and when are they needed?

> Biocompatibility testing services assess whether materials used in medical devices are safe and compatible with biological systems. These tests are vital before regulatory submission to confirm that the product will not cause any adverse biological reactions in patients.

### 12.6 Is Virologica accredited for its testing methods?

> Yes, Virologica holds ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation from UKAS for ISO 18184 antiviral activity of textiles and ASTM D7907 bactericidal activity of medical gloves. This means our methods and results are independently verified to meet the highest international standards of quality and reliability.

### 12.7 How can I get started with Virologica’s viral or microbiology testing services?

> Getting started is simple—just contact our team. Once your project begins, a dedicated lead scientist from our virus testing lab will guide you through each step, ensuring your testing is delivered accurately and on time.
